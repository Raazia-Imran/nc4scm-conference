This is an information website. Sending an enquiry does not register attendance, submit a paper, reserve a room or make a payment. Opening soon, TBC and Preview mean the details are still awaiting confirmation.

## 3. Reading the poster and preparing a paper

Open Call for Papers. Select the poster to open its full-size image in a separate tab; use the browser zoom to read details. If the image does not load, the page displays a fallback message. The developer should investigate the asset rather than asking visitors to upload it.

Download the GCGC Paper Template (DOCM) and Springer Nature Proceedings Guidelines (PDF) from Call for Papers, Home resources or the footer. These downloads help with preparation; they do not submit the paper or guarantee publication. The body of the template/guidelines was not audited for publisher requirements in this checkpoint. Follow the actual approved author instructions when the organizers finalize them.

The site currently describes an eight-stage EasyChair workflow but has no dedicated GCGC submission URL. Do not search for or use an unverified portal as though it were official. Ask the conference team through the official address when the link becomes available.

## 4. Tracks and archive

On Home, select a track card to read its research areas; use the close control to return. On Call for Papers, expand a research-track section. Three tracks contain nine areas spanning materials, sustainable construction, and case studies/best practices.

Open Archive to browse 2025 photographs. Select a photograph to open its larger view; use the gallery controls to move or close. The developer should preserve accurate captions and avoid presenting old photographs as photographs of the future conference.

## 5. Sending an enquiry

Open Contact. Enter your name, email, enquiry category and message. Name and message must contain more than spaces. Source limits are 120 characters for name, 254 for email and 5,000 for message. Submit only appropriate conference information; avoid passwords, identity documents and payment details.

Submission leaves the site for FormSubmit's spam check/result page. Complete any requested provider CAPTCHA and read the actual result. A frontend click alone does not prove mailbox delivery. If there is an error or no confirmation, email nc4scm@cloud.neduet.edu.pk directly using your own email application.

## 6. Privacy settings

Open Privacy settings in the footer or on Cookies and storage. Performance monitoring is off by default. Save permission if you want to allow optional Vercel Speed Insights, or choose Essential only to keep it disabled. Closing the dialog without saving keeps the current choice.

A saved browser choice lasts up to 180 days. Withdrawing previous permission reloads the page to stop the loaded monitoring code. If storage is blocked or invalid, monitoring stays off. This is a source-described behavior; the hosting dashboard and actual production measurements were not checked in this session. The site has no application account or payment feature.

## 7. Giving approved updates to the developer

Send the exact page/section, final wording, effective date, original image/document/link, approving person and whether old content must stay. Do not send passwords or tokens in the content pack. For names and biographies, supply approved spelling, affiliation, role, portrait and permission. For logos/photos, provide originals and usage approval; filenames alone are not proof of rights.

The conference has no separate general-purpose content editor or poster Studio. Most changes require a source update and deployment. The two keynote profiles use the separate NC4SCM public Sanity event at build time, so a CMS edit still needs a conference rebuild before it appears. The developer should handle that coordination; visitors do not need developer accounts.
